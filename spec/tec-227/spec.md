# ERP System for Small Company with Leave Management

## Summary
Design and develop a comprehensive ERP system for companies with fewer than 100 employees, including core business modules and leave management functionality. A team member is actively drafting user stories and acceptance criteria, incorporating feedback to add leave management requirements.

## Approved Story and Acceptance Criteria
## User Story
As a small business owner with fewer than 100 employees, I want a comprehensive ERP system that manages all core business operations including leave management in one integrated platform so that I can streamline processes, reduce manual work, and gain real-time visibility into my company's performance without the complexity and cost of enterprise-level solutions.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] System supports user management for up to 100 employee accounts with role-based access controls and permission levels appropriate for small business hierarchies
- [ ] Core modules include financial management (accounting, invoicing, expenses), inventory management, customer relationship management (CRM), and human resources functionality accessible from a unified dashboard
- [ ] Leave management module allows employees to submit leave requests, managers to approve or deny requests, tracks leave balances by type (vacation, sick, personal), and maintains leave calendar visibility across the organization
- [ ] System generates standard business reports including profit and loss statements, inventory levels, sales summaries, employee reports, and leave reports with data export capabilities to common formats (PDF, Excel, CSV)
- [ ] All business data persists securely with automated daily backups and maintains data integrity across all integrated modules with real-time synchronization
- [ ] User interface follows accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA) with responsive design that works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices for remote and on-site access, and system handles common error scenarios gracefully with clear user messaging and audit trails for all transactions
